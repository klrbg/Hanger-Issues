# HANGER ISSUES

**Minimum wage. Maximum rage.**

*A first-person retail simulator inspired by the unfiltered reality of working in retail — the endless mess, unreasonable customers, impossible expectations, and everyday absurdities that retail workers know all too well.*

**Status: Playable Prototype | Work in Progress**

## About the Game

HANGER ISSUES is an independent 3D game project inspired by my six years of professional experience as a Visual Merchandiser in retail.

The game aims to combine everyday clothing store responsibilities with absurd humor, unpredictable situations and player-driven chaos.

Players will be able to choose whether to become responsible employees or embrace the chaos of retail life.

## Current Playable Prototype

The current prototype demonstrates a small but functional gameplay loop centered around cleaning fitting rooms and hanging clothing.

**Implemented features:**

- First-person movement and camera controls
- Clothing pickup, carrying and dropping
- Physics-based object interactions
- SphereCast-based object detection
- Clothing rack with three hanging slots
- Task progress tracking
- Shift completion and restart
- Custom 3D shirt, hanger and clothing rack models made in Blender
- Basic store environment, materials and lighting

**Current gameplay loop:**

Collect clothing → Carry it to the rack → Hang it → Complete the shift.

## Planned Gameplay

The full game is envisioned around a series of increasingly chaotic retail shifts.

Planned mechanics include:

- **Fitting Room Cleanup:** Collect abandoned clothing from changing rooms.
- **Clothing Sorting:** Organize garments by category, size or department.
- **Fix & Rehang:** Correct improperly hung clothing and clean up fallen items.
- **Return to Sales Floor:** Transport and distribute clothing to the appropriate store sections.
- **Customer Chaos:** Handle interruptions, complaints and unpredictable customer behavior.
- **Player Freedom:** Complete tasks responsibly, ignore them or deliberately create chaos — with consequences.

### Shift-Based Progression

Instead of traditional levels, the planned game will introduce new challenges through increasingly demanding work shifts.

Future shifts may introduce sorting, restocking, customer interactions and more complex consequences for player decisions.

These mechanics and progression systems are planned features, not yet part of the current playable prototype.
